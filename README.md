# 🎮 Gamer's Hub — Premium Arcade & Strategy Suite

A high-fidelity browser game arcade featuring 15 classic and modern mini-games, smooth animations, sleek glassmorphism aesthetics, a persistent Hall of Fame, and a built-in **Arcade Coach & Strategy System**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **15 Integrated Games**:
  1. **Tactical Toe**: 3×3 duel against an unbeatable minimax AI or local Player 2.
  2. **Cyber Crush**: Neon match-3 puzzle with cascades and combo multipliers.
  3. **Memory Pulse**: Fast-paced card matching with custom difficulty options.
  4. **Cyber 2048**: Smooth tile-sliding logic puzzle to reach 2048.
  5. **Snake Quest**: Race to 100 with dynamic snakes and ladders (1-4 players).
  6. **Ludo Legends**: Full 4-player board strategy with dice rolling and safe squares.
  7. **Sudoku Nexus**: 9×9 logical number puzzle with candidate notes and error checking.
  8. **Royal Queens**: Classic 8 Queens puzzle on an 8×8 chessboard.
  9. **Slide Master**: 15-puzzle sliding block challenge.
  10. **Word Climb**: Morph words one letter at a time via dictionary lookups.
  11. **Logic Tango**: Binary 0/1 (Sun/Moon) grid logic puzzle (Takuzu).
  12. **Speed Zip**: Reflex-testing dynamic target tapping game.
  13. **Math Sprint**: Rapid mental math challenge against the clock.
  14. **Color Fill**: Strategic flood-fill puzzle with move budgets.
  15. **Connections**: Word-association puzzle finding 4 groups of 4.

- **🧠 Integrated Arcade Coach & Strategy Guide**:
  - Accessible directly from the main header (**🧠 COACH & STRATEGY**) or inside any live game (**🧠 COACH TIPS**).
  - Complete rules recap, prioritized winning strategies, common mistakes to avoid, and long-term practice drills.
  - **Arcade Cheat Sheet**: High-impact top 3 winning tips for all 15 games on a single screen.

- **🏆 Hall of Fame & Profiles**:
  - Custom gamer tags and local score persistence.
  - Global rankings and game-specific win counters.

- **🎨 Premium Visuals**:
  - Glassmorphism design system with dark-mode aesthetic and vibrant neon gradients.
  - Confetti particle celebration engine for victories.
  - Keyboard shortcut support (`Esc` to back out or close modals).

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser.
2. Or spin up a local development server:
   ```bash
   npx serve .
   ```

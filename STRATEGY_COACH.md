## 🎮 Game-by-Game Strategy Dossiers

### 1. Tactical Toe
- **Rules Recap**: 3×3 duel alternating X and O. First to get 3 in a row wins. Unbeatable AI guarantees a draw under perfect play.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Claim center or opposite corners on move 1 to maximize branching lines.
  2. Block any opponent two-in-a-row immediately.
  3. Form dual-threat forks (two simultaneous winning paths) to punish misplays.
- **Common Mistakes**: Opening on edge squares; ignoring opponent diagonal forks; expecting easy wins vs minimax AI.
- **Practice Tip**: Drill all 8 standard board responses until drawing as O becomes second nature.

### 2. Cyber Crush
- **Rules Recap**: Swap adjacent gems to make matches of 3+. Cleared gems disappear and new gems fall down. 4- and 5-matches yield special power and cascading scores.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Execute matches at the bottom of the board to trigger free cascading clears.
  2. Prioritize 4- and 5-gem alignments over basic 3-matches.
  3. Scan bottom-up so you don't ruin upper combos before triggering them.
- **Common Mistakes**: Playing exclusively near the top; rushing moves without checking for 4-in-a-row setups.
- **Practice Tip**: Pause 3 seconds before every swap to verify if an existing cluster can be upgraded into a 4-gem clear.

### 3. Memory Pulse
- **Rules Recap**: Timed card-matching puzzle. Flip 2 cards per turn; matching pairs stay revealed. Clear the entire board before the clock reaches zero.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Map the grid by spatial zones (quadrants or rows) rather than clicking arbitrarily.
  2. Associate each card with a rapid mental tag or coordinate.
  3. Always uncover an unknown tile first on move 1, then match with a known tile on move 2.
- **Common Mistakes**: Clicking blindly when timer runs low; turning over known tiles without knowing their pair.
- **Practice Tip**: Practice calling out row/column coordinates (e.g. R2C3) to train instant visual indexing.

### 4. Cyber 2048
- **Rules Recap**: 4×4 grid sliding puzzle. All tiles shift in the chosen direction; identical numbers merge into their sum. Reach 2048 before the board fills up.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Anchor your highest number tile in a permanent corner (e.g. bottom-left).
  2. Build a monotonic descending snake along the wall leading into your anchor.
  3. Strictly avoid swiping in the direction away from your corner (e.g. Up).
- **Common Mistakes**: Swiping aimlessly in all 4 directions; letting your king row get infiltrated by low 2/4 spawns.
- **Practice Tip**: Play whole games using strictly 3 direction keys to ingrain corner discipline.

### 5. Snake Quest
- **Rules Recap**: Race from tile 1 to 100 via die rolls. Ladders boost you upward; snake heads pull you backward. First to reach 100 wins.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Monitor danger zones 1 to 6 squares ahead of your position each turn.
  2. Keep emotional composure early; high-tier ladders (tile 28 to 84) can overturn huge deficits.
  3. Target safe squares when maneuvering through the perilous 80-99 endgame zone.
- **Common Mistakes**: Tilting after an early snake bite; losing track of opponent ladder windows.
- **Practice Tip**: Memorize the board's highest-impact ladder paths to calculate realistic board momentum.

### 6. Ludo Legends
- **Rules Recap**: Guide 4 tokens around the board. A roll of 6 releases a token from base and grants a bonus roll. Capture opponent tokens to reset them to base. Reach home column with all 4 tokens to win.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Deploy all 4 tokens out of base promptly to ensure diverse move options on every roll.
  2. Park tokens on colored/star safe squares and use them as ambush pads.
  3. Avoid stranding solo tokens 1-6 squares directly in front of opponents.
- **Common Mistakes**: Racing a single token while leaving 3 trapped in base; bypassing safe squares when threatened.
- **Practice Tip**: Constantly measure 1-6 roll distances between your tokens and trailing enemies.

### 7. Sudoku Nexus
- **Rules Recap**: Fill the 9×9 grid so digits 1-9 appear once in every row, column, and 3×3 box.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Find naked singles where 8 other numbers are already present in row, column, and box.
  2. Find hidden singles where a digit has only one legal home in a 3×3 region.
  3. Cross-hatch numbers across parallel 3×3 grids to locate quick placements.
- **Common Mistakes**: Guessing numbers; checking row and column while forgetting to check the 3×3 box.
- **Practice Tip**: Start every puzzle cross-hatching digits 1 through 9 sequentially.

### 8. Royal Queens
- **Rules Recap**: Place 8 queens on an 8×8 board so no two queens attack each other horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Place exactly one queen per column/row using knight-move step offsets.
  2. Avoid placing queens in immediate board corners or centers in opening moves.
  3. Progress column-by-column from left to right; backtrack immediately if a column has 0 safe cells.
- **Common Mistakes**: Placing queens without diagonal line-of-sight checks; hesitating to undo 2 steps back.
- **Practice Tip**: Memorize the opening 4-column knight-step offsets.

### 9. Slide Master
- **Rules Recap**: Slide numbered tiles into the empty space to arrange tiles in numerical order (1-8 or 1-15) with the blank in the bottom-right.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Solve row-by-row top-to-bottom: lock Row 1, then Row 2, then solve the remaining bottom.
  2. For the last 2 tiles of a row (e.g. 3 & 4), position tile 4 beneath tile 3 and rotate together.
  3. Never dismantle a solved upper row to move lower tiles.
- **Common Mistakes**: Moving tiles randomly without a 3-step cycle plan; forcing corner tiles directly.
- **Practice Tip**: Master the tandem corner-tuck maneuver (dock N-1, position N beneath, rotate into line).

### 10. Word Climb
- **Rules Recap**: Transform the start word into the goal word by changing 1 letter per step. Each step must form a valid dictionary word.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Compare start and target words to isolate differing positions.
  2. Bridge transitions using high-frequency vowels (A, E, O) and versatile consonants (S, T, R).
  3. Work bidirectionally: take one step backward from the goal word if the forward route stalls.
- **Common Mistakes**: Using obscure words with rare letters (Q, X, Z) that lead to dead ends; changing already-matched letters.
- **Practice Tip**: List all 1-letter rhyming mutations before committing to a path.

### 11. Logic Tango
- **Rules Recap**: Fill binary grid with 0s and 1s. Maximum 2 adjacent identical symbols per line. Equal count of 0s and 1s in every row and column. No duplicate lines.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Cap doublets immediately (00 becomes 1001) to prevent 3-in-a-row.
  2. Fill sandwiches (0_0 becomes 010).
  3. Count line quotas: once a line has its limit of 0s, fill all remaining cells with 1s.
- **Common Mistakes**: Permitting 3 identical symbols in a line; guessing before counting row/column quotas.
- **Practice Tip**: Use two-pass sweeps: Pass 1 resolves doublets/sandwiches; Pass 2 resolves line quotas.

### 12. Speed Zip
- **Rules Recap**: Rapid reaction game. Tap dynamic appearing targets before their timer expires. Faster reflexes award higher multipliers.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Always return mouse/finger to the center of the arena after every tap.
  2. Employ soft peripheral vision covering the full arena rather than staring at the last hit.
  3. Keep finger relaxed; physical tension adds 40-60ms of lag.
- **Common Mistakes**: Resting cursor on screen edges; spamming frantic double-clicks.
- **Practice Tip**: Drill instant cursor re-centering drills between rounds.

### 13. Math Sprint
- **Rules Recap**: Timed mental arithmetic test. Solve arithmetic equations under clock pressure. Accuracy and speed both drive score.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Inspect the last digit (units digit) of the equation to eliminate wrong options immediately.
  2. Deconstruct numbers into round base-10 parts (e.g. 19×7 = (20×7) - 7 = 133).
  3. Round and compensate for tricky subtractions.
- **Common Mistakes**: Computing the complete calculation when choices have unique final digits; rushing and breaking multiplier streaks.
- **Practice Tip**: Practice rapid units-digit tables (e.g., 6×7 ends in 2; 8+7 ends in 5).

### 14. Color Fill
- **Rules Recap**: Flood-fill grid from top-left origin. Choose colors to absorb adjacent tiles of matching color. Unify the board within the move budget.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Pick the color that absorbs the largest perimeter frontier on each turn.
  2. Path toward large single-color clusters situated in the board interior.
  3. Expand outward along the diagonal toward the bottom-right corner.
- **Common Mistakes**: Picking colors that only annex 1 tile; re-selecting the active flood color.
- **Practice Tip**: Count candidate border cells before each tap to verify positive net gain.

### 15. Connections
- **Rules Recap**: Organize 16 words into 4 distinct groups of 4 sharing a category. Only 4 mistakes allowed.
- **Best Strategy**:
  1. Watch for 5-word traps: if 5 words fit a category, identify the intruder before guessing.
  2. Look for wordplay (fill-in-the-blanks, rhymes, palindromes, homophones).
  3. Lock in the tightest, most specific category first to eliminate overlap noise.
- **Common Mistakes**: Falling for obvious red herrings; stubbornly repeating guesses on the same failing group.
- **Practice Tip**: Cluster potential 4-packs on paper or in notes before submitting your first move.
